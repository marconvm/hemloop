# Hemloop video cards

- **workflow:** motion-graphics
- **flow:** autonomous
- **deliverable:** One HyperFrames composition — intro card (2.5 s) + seven step cards (1.2 s each) on Hemloop tokens
- **tokens:** paper `#f4f0e6`, ink `#17211c`, lime `#b9f227`, animated ring+dot mark
- **copy:** Intro — `Hemloop · one request, all the way round · built on WebMCP`. Steps — `Step N of 7 · <rail label>` (New item, Local demand, Approved request, Matched offer, Bought, Learned, Again)
- **motion:** label rises 12 px; lime dot orbits the ring once per card
- **output:** `out/cards-landscape.mp4` (1920×1080) and `out/cards-portrait.mp4` (1080×1920), 30 fps, paper background
- **cue:** `video/CUE-SHEET.md` — intro + each step card sits on the cut into that step
