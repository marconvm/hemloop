# Use cases

Five shapes of the same loop. Each page is linked from the **Use cases** group in the sidebar —
one screenshot, one flow map.

| Page | Story |
|---|---|
| [Right time](./uc-right-time.md) | Purchase dates turn into `replace` demand the merchant's CRM cannot see. |
| [What the merchant cannot fill](./uc-cannot-fill.md) | Market scan + verdict table against locked stock. |
| [Shopping for someone else](./uc-family.md) | Me / Partner / Kid scopes tools; `for` only at level 2+. |
| [A receipt from a rival](./uc-rival-receipt.md) | Local import sharpens pattern; only the derived shape can leave at level 3. |
| [Handoff to a shopping agent](./uc-handoff.md) | `get_offer` + completeness meter for a third-party agent. |

Personas are synthetic; merchants and offers are fictional. Wardrobe brand photos are real (see
[PHOTO-CREDITS](./PHOTO-CREDITS.md)).
